Frequently asked

Why does a frame number start with “≈”? Exact frame times couldn't be read from that file, so numbers are estimated from its frame rate. Stepping still works.

Why is the saved frame not as sharp as a photo? It is a frame from a video, so its quality is limited by the video's resolution and compression.

Why do HDR videos look different in GIFs? GIF supports 256 colors per frame, so HDR is converted to SDR.

Where did my recent videos go? If the original was deleted or moved, choose it again from Photos or Files.
